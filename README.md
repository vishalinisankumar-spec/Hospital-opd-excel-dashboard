# 🏥 Hospital OPD Patient Flow Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing outpatient (OPD) visits, revenue, and patient experience across departments and doctors.



## 📌 Overview
- **Dataset:** 2,698 OPD visits | Jan–Jun 2026 | 8 departments | 14 doctors
- **Note:** The dataset is simulated for portfolio practice and does not contain real patient records.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills
- Excel PivotTables & PivotCharts
- Slicers (Month, Department)
- KPI cards (formulas linked to shapes)
- Data cleaning (duplicate check, handling blanks)
- Dashboard design & formatting


## 💡 Key Insights
- **General Medicine** generates the highest revenue (₹3.3L) and the most visits.
- **Cardiology** has fewer visits than Pediatrics but higher revenue, due to a higher fee per visit.
- **UPI** is the most used payment mode, at about 41% of revenue.
- About **8% of patients are no-shows**, which is an opportunity to reduce lost appointments with reminders.
